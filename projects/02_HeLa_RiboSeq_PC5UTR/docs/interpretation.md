# Interpretation of PC5UTR RPF Signals

This analysis evaluates ribosome-protected fragment coverage across candidate PC5UTR / 5′UTR regions in two uninduced HeLa Ribo-seq replicates.

Regions such as SRSF2, VIM, and TOP2B show stronger RPF density and are prioritized for further visualization and periodicity analysis.

Current evidence should be interpreted as ribosome occupancy rather than definitive proof of active translation.

Further validation requires:

- IGV visualization
- P-site offset analysis
- 3-nt periodicity
- uORF annotation
- RNA-seq-normalized translation efficiency analysis
- matched background 5′UTR control regions
